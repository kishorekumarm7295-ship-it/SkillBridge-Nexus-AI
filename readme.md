# 🎓 SkillBridge Nexus AI

## Academia - Industry Collaboration Portal

A smart AI-powered platform that connects:

- Students
- Industries
- Academicians
- Institutions

to bridge the gap between academic skills and industry requirements.


## 🚀 Problem Statement

Students often lack awareness about industry-required skills and career opportunities.

Industries struggle to find skilled candidates.

SkillBridge Nexus AI provides a unified ecosystem for skill assessment, internship, placement and collaboration.
